  • 📊 Consumer sentiment mining – NLP scrapes 50M+ online conversations to spot "unarticulated needs."
  • 📈 Demand forecasting – Machine learning predicts regional adoption curves for new products.

📖 Scenario: "The Fabric Softener Pivot"

In 2022, P&G's AI flagged a rising conversation cluster in Southeast Asia: consumers complained that traditional fabric softeners left a "waxy residue" on sportswear. The AI cross-referenced this with sales data and found a 12% decline in softener sales in humid cities.

Instead of launching a new product from scratch, P&G used their AI to reformulate an existing brand (Downy) – adding a moisture-wicking micro-coating that works with synthetic fabrics. The AI simulated 8,000+ chemical combinations in 72 hours – a process that used to take 18 months.

Result: A new "Downy Sport" variant launched in 9 months (vs. 24-month average). Within 6 months, it captured 8% market share in the segment.

🤔 But here's the tension: The AI also suggested two alternative formulations – one cheaper (lower margins) and one premium (higher margins but slower adoption). P&G's innovation team disagreed on which to pursue.

🎯 Key Takeaways from This Activity

  • AI is a powerful accelerator – but it does not replace human judgement, ethics, or empathy.
  • Innovation teams need cross-functional dialogue – R&D, marketing, sustainability, and legal must co-decide.
  • Data-driven decisions are only as good as the questions we ask the data.
  • ESG and AI are not separate – they are two sides of the same coin in modern innovation.
