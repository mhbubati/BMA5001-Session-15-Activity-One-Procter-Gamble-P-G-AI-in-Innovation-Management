🏢 Procter & Gamble (P&G) – AI in Innovation

A Real-World Case Study for Innovation Management

📖 Introduction

Procter & Gamble (P&G) is one of the world's largest consumer goods companies. You know their products: Tide laundry detergent, Pampers nappies, Gillette razors, Oral-B toothbrushes, and Olay skincare.

P&G has been using data to understand customers for over 100 years. In 1924, the CEO asked an economist to find out how customers used Ivory soap. The result? Ivory was repositioned as a personal care product instead of a household item.

"We've always had a drive for analytics and to understand the dynamics behind our business. But with AI, the nature of questions we can ask and the depth of answers we can provide have expanded dramatically."
– Jeff Goldman, VP of Enterprise Data Science, P&G

Today, P&G is using analytical, generative, and agentic AI to develop new products, optimise supply chains, and transform R&D.

🏢 P&G's AI Applications

🔧 The "AI Factory"

P&G developed an "AI Factory" – a platform that provides data sources, software tools, methods, and security protocols to rapidly develop, test, deploy, and monitor AI algorithms.

Metric Result
Time saved Reduces model deployment by ~6 months
Speed On the manufacturing floor, AI cuts model deployment time by up to 90%
Impact In Brazil, AI reduced out-of-stock occurrences by 15%
💡 Key Takeaway: The "AI Factory" is not just a tool — it is an infrastructure that makes AI scalable across the entire organisation.

🍼 Pampers My Perfect Fit

An AI-driven questionnaire that provides parents with diaper fit recommendations that are 90% accurate for preventing leaks – the most common pain point in the market.

👃 Perfume Development Digital Suite

An ecosystem of digital tools powered by AI and advanced data processing to create new fragrances five times faster than previously possible.

Key Insight: AI algorithms analyse millions of data points and help create perfume character models based on consumer insights into what makes a fragrance smell good.

💡 Great Idea Generator

An AI tool that creates product and advertising concepts based on consumer trends and previous testing results. It can greatly accelerate the progress of a new idea, from concept to advertisement to shelf.

💬 insightsPG

A generative front-end to business data. As P&G's CIO put it:

"Why should you need a dashboard when you can talk to your data?"

👥 Project Genie

An AI tool that synthesises information from articles and help documents to provide information to over 800 customer service reps, greatly reducing question processing time.

🧪 R&D Acceleration

AI is complementing the work of lab technicians to enable greater speed in molecular discovery. This is helping break down functional silos between R&D and commercial professionals.

🧪 The "Cybernetic Teammate" Experiment

P&G conducted a large-scale field experiment with 776 professionals across four business units.

The Four Models Tested

Model Description
1. Individual, no AI Baseline – one person working alone
2. Team, no AI Traditional cross-functional teamwork
3. Individual + AI One person with AI support
4. Team + AI Cross-functional team with AI – ⭐ BEST RESULTS

The Results

Finding Detail
Individuals with AI Matched the performance of teams without AI
Teams with AI Produced the best outcomes – ideas ranking in the top 10% were 3 times more likely to come from AI-augmented teams
Breaking silos R&D professionals using AI produced balanced solutions (not just technical), while commercial professionals produced balanced solutions (not just commercial)
Emotional impact Individuals with AI showed 46% increase in positive emotions; AI-augmented teams showed 64% boost
"If you want to empower an individual to be as effective as a team, give them AI. But if you want to be in that top 10% of performers, a full human team plus AI seems like the recipe for success."
– Fabrizio Dell'Acqua, Harvard Business School

🔑 Key Lessons from P&G

Lesson P&G's Approach
Build the right infrastructure The "AI Factory" reduces deployment time by 6 months
Keep humans in the loop "AI is a means, not an end. Be clear on what end you're trying to achieve."
– Alfredo Colas, SVP IT North America
Upskill your workforce Over 4,000 executives have completed an intensive 8-week AI upskilling program
Democratise access "Why should you need a dashboard when you can talk to your data?" – InsightsPG
Treat AI as a teammate The "cybernetic teammate" concept – AI is a partner, not a replacement
🎯 The Big Picture: P&G's success comes from integrating AI into the organisation – not just buying tools, but building culture, infrastructure, and skills around it.
